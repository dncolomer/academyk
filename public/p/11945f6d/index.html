Course proposal

Quantum Error Correction: Diagnosis, Simulation, and Engineering

Learners work with imperfect systems. They inject errors, look at what goes wrong, and try to fix it. Through a running project, the Decoder Co-Design and Benchmarking Lab, they build and refine a working QEC system from noise models to final performance claims.

Running project

Decoder Co-Design and Benchmarking Lab

Learners maintain one evolving system throughout the course: a fixed noise model paired with a small stabilizer code. They instrument syndrome extraction, start with a basic decoder, and successively replace or strengthen it while measuring logical error rate against latency and overhead. At each stage they diagnose why a change improves or degrades performance under the given noise.
